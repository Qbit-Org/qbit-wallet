Refuse a restore floor read from address statistics that an explorer could have inflated. A restored address whose history is too long to enumerate takes its signature-counter floor from the explorer's address statistics, and inflated but self-consistent statistics could set that floor as high as the whole 2^30 budget. No path lowers a floor, so the account could not sign until the wallet was restored again. The statistics read now fails, as its other consistency checks do, when a scope lists more funded or spent outputs than its transactions can carry. A transaction fits in the 2,000,000-byte block, so it carries at most 46,511 outputs paying a P2MR address and 536 inputs spending one. The read also fails when the floor reaches the 2^28-signature warning threshold, and so does the restore floor scan when testnet4 and regtest floors, which share a counter, reach it together and either came from statistics. Either refusal fails the floor scan, so `pqcFloorsPending` stays set, signing stays locked and the scan can be retried against an honest explorer. A floor below the threshold, from statistics within those bounds, is accepted as before. `@qbit/keyring-service` now exports `PQC_WARNING_SIGNATURE_THRESHOLD`, so the wallet reads the threshold from one place. No vault field changes.
