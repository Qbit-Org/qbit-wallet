Lock when you step away. `WalletController.lockOnSystemEvent('computer-locked' | 'windows-closed')` locks the wallet when that event's setting is on (`getLockOnComputerLock`, `getLockOnWindowsClosed` and their setters, also callable from the wallet's pages). It does nothing before the wallet's vault exists, so a lock during first-time onboarding cannot strand the user. A lock wins over an unlock that is still waiting its turn or running: `unlock` notes the locks made since it was called and then rejects with `WALLET_LOCKED_DURING_UNLOCK`, leaving the wallet locked. The auto-lock now also keeps a wall-clock deadline, because a timer does not count the time the computer sleeps: a screen action (`setLastActiveTime`) or a change of the computer's idle state (`lockIfAutoLockOverdue`) after the auto-lock time locks the wallet instead of extending the session. Any lock ends the auto-lock time, so an earlier timer can no longer lock a later unlock or report an auto-lock for a wallet already locked. A broadcast already under way still settles after a lock.
