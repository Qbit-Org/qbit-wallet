Add `planQbitSend`, which plans a payment that needs more of an account's outputs than one transaction carries: merges that each spend up to 106 of the account's confirmed outputs and pay one output back to the account, then the one payment that spends every merge's output and the outputs left over. Every size, fee and amount in a `QbitSendPlan` is exact: each merge's inputs, fee and output value, the payment's inputs, amount, fee and change, the total fee, the number of signatures and the outputs left behind. For an amount, a plan spends the fewest of the largest outputs that pay it, and so the fewest merges; for everything, the outputs that give the recipient the most. A plan never spends unconfirmed outputs and has no more merges than one payment can spend (106 for one recipient output). When no plan pays the target, `planQbitSend` returns a `QbitSendPlanRefusal` (`insufficient_balance`, `exceeds_plan_size`, `exceeds_maximum_total_fee` or `nothing_to_send`) with the largest amount a plan can send, and it throws a `RangeError` for a fee rate outside the policy, a malformed amount or output, and an output listed twice. `isQbitSendPlanAmount` accepts a plan's amount: a safe integer of at least 2,855 bits (#188).

Add the shapes a send plan crosses the UI port in (`types/qbit-send-plan.ts`): `QbitSendPlanView`, the plan as the wallet's pages show it, with its phase, the reason it is paused, the actions it offers, whether a transaction of it (or one a fee raise replaced) was sent or may be in the network (`sent`: what cancelling leaves there, its fee paid), whether a version of its payment left the wallet without a verdict and may still confirm (`paymentMayConfirm`, only ever `true`: the plan can then be neither cancelled nor reviewed again, and the recipient may still be paid by it) and a revision that only grows; `QbitSendPlanReview`, the whole plan priced before anything is signed; the parameters and results of preparing and starting one, including a revision of an open plan (`QbitSendPlanRevision`) and the refusal `fee_rate_too_low` with the lowest rate that works. `QbitFeeBumpUnavailableReason` gains `held_by_send_plan`: a send plan holds an input of the transaction, as a completed plan holds its payment's until the payment confirms or the plan is dismissed, so Speed up is not offered for it. `BUS_METHODS.QBIT_SEND_PLAN_CHANGED` tells every wallet page a plan changed, and `estimateQbitSendPlanSigningSeconds` states how long signing a plan is expected to take (#188).
