Sign every reviewed plan of a recovery migration in one press (#156). `KeyringService.openQbitRecoveryMigrationSigningBatch(address, inputs)` opens the signature batch of one source key's plans that a migration review shows; every entry must be a prepared plan of a migration of that key. The same plans with the same approved budgets and restored-floor challenges reopen the same batch, so a review prepared again does not open another. Such a batch is never evicted, so a burst of other signing requests cannot close it while its review is open: with 64 batches open and none of them a local batch, opening another is refused. It closes when its review releases the restored-floor challenge of any of its plans (`releaseQbitRestoredFloorSigningChallenges`, as a review that ends or is replaced does), on a lock, when a later batch opens for the same key's plans before any entry of it signed, and once none of its plans can sign any more: when its last plan signs, or when the challenges of all the plans left have expired, as after a review left without releasing them. A challenge consumed as its plan signs leaves the batch open for the other plans, and releasing a challenge closes no other kind of batch. The result's `created` tells a batch opened by the call from one it reopened. The scope these batches use is reserved: `openP2mrPsbtSigningBatch` refuses it. An entry of a batch that is no longer open is now refused with `QbitSignatureBatchInterruptedError` (code `QBIT_SIGNATURE_BATCH_INTERRUPTED`, exported as `QBIT_SIGNATURE_BATCH_INTERRUPTED_CODE`; message unchanged), thrown before any of the entry is signed.
