Skip the output listing on a balance poll of an unchanged account with many outputs. `getQbitSpendableBalanceV2` takes a `reuse` option (`QbitBalanceReadOptions.reuse`), the read's place among the answers a `QbitBalanceReuse` keeps (`begin`), which the controller passes from `getAddressBalanceV2`; with a client other than the wallet's own (`QbitWalletEsploraClient`, `QbitWalletMempoolClient`), which alone ask for a read fresh, nothing is kept or reused. The classified outputs of a complete read, of a listing of at least `QBIT_BALANCE_REUSE_MIN_OUTPUTS` (1,000) outputs, every one confirmed, that agrees in count and value with the confirmed statistics read with it, no mempool number of which is above 0, at a tip that answered, each output classified as paying the address's own script (an answer naming another script serves its one read, and the shared reader keeps no fact of it), are kept as a candidate, provided no fact the shared reader keeps changed or went from before the read classified any until it ended (`QbitFundingTxReads.factChanges`, new: per client, how many times a kept fact was replaced by one that differs, pushed out or forgotten, or every fact cleared; it never goes back). A later read, begun after it was kept, that lists the same outputs (`txid:vout`, value and height of each, compared by a SHA-256 digest) under the same statistics and the same count, in a listing asked for fresh (under a URL no other request has used, which no cache answers and no read in flight shares), confirms it, and that read's own answer is reused for at most `QBIT_BALANCE_REUSE_MAX_AGE_MS` (5 minutes) from when it began, by the monotonic and the wall clock alike, and only while the count has not moved. A poll with an answer to reuse asks for the statistics, fresh, and the tip, and no listing: it reuses the answer when the statistics read as they did for it and the tip neither fell below the one it was read at nor reached the height at which an output it counts immature matures, with the send guard's reservations read afresh, and its split is what a read of the listing gives. Otherwise it reads the listing once the statistics have answered, one round trip later, and the reservations again after it; it takes the count again before that listing, so that a fact changed during the poll does not stop what it lists from being kept as a candidate, which the next poll may confirm.

- An account under 1,000 listed outputs, and any poll with no answer kept for its address, sends the requests it sent before, with the same timing. A poll with a candidate kept, or an answer past its age, asks for the listing fresh, with the statistics.
- A poll with an answer to reuse waits for the tip at most `QBIT_BALANCE_REUSE_TIP_WAIT_MS` (1 s) once the statistics have answered. When the tip fails, or does not answer in time, the poll reads the listing as a poll with nothing kept does and sets the answer aside: until its age runs out, polls of the address read as with nothing kept, so a slow or broken tip costs one late poll in 5 minutes at most. A listing read with no tip height forgets what is kept.
- Statistics that changed or show a payment in the mempool, a kept fact that changed or went, and a listing that may not be kept forget the kept answer, so the next poll asks for the listing with the statistics again. A read of outputs for a send that corrects a fact the explorer answered wrongly before ends the reuse at once, whether that read completes or not, and so does a fact pushed out of the full shared reader (65,536 outputs) or dropped for disagreeing with a listing; a read that changes nothing leaves it.
- The controller forgets the answers wherever it forgets the kept funding facts (a lock, a reset, a keyring removal, a network switch, dropped regtest clients), and again as a keyring removal ends, after which none is held in memory; nothing is kept or reused while the wallet reads locked. Its split is unknown when its last read of the reservations in a poll found the send guard not answering within its wait.
- What remains: a listing that lags while its count and sum still equal the statistics, repeated identically by two fresh reads, or one that changed under the same statistics (a reorganisation at the same height swapping a payment for another of the same value), is reused for at most 5 minutes or until the statistics change; and balance reads of the account that overlap while a candidate is kept, or an answer past its age (the poll after a change, and the one that confirms the answer again every 5 minutes), each download the listing, where they shared one request before.
