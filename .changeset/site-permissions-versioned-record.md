'@qbit/permission-service': patch
---

Store connected-site permissions in a record the wallet owns, `{ version: 1, sites }` with the most recently used site first, instead of lru-cache 6's internal dump, and drop the lru-cache dependency. `PermissionService.init` still reads the dump that 1.0.6 and earlier wrote, with every grant, chain, pin, signing and quick multi-sign setting in it, and the record is rewritten in the new form on the next change to a site rather than at start-up. A site entry that 1.0.6 kept under a key other than its own origin is kept under that key, which is the origin 1.0.6 checked the grant by. A record that is not an object, has a version this release does not know or has no list of sites is rejected as a whole: the service logs why, does not throw, and starts with no site connected, so each site has to connect again. Within a record that can be read, a site with a missing or wrongly typed field, an expiry, a key that differs from its origin and is not an origin itself, or an origin that appears more than once is dropped on its own and logged, and the other sites keep their grants. A change that would store such a site is rolled back and rejects instead of being written. `updateConnectSite` keeps a site under the origin it was given, even when the new value names another. The service still keeps at most 1000 sites and drops the least recently used.

A change to a site made before `init` has loaded the stored record now rejects instead of resolving without storing anything. `resetAllData` and `clearAllPermissions` store the empty record even when `init` could not read the stored one, so a reset no longer leaves grants on disk for the next start, and they reject when that write fails.

The package index now also exports the `LRUCacheDumpStore` and `LRUEntry` types.

Breaking change: the exported `PermissionStore` type now describes the versioned record, and the dump's shape is `LRUCacheDumpStore`. Once this release has rewritten the record, a downgrade to 1.0.6 reads no grants, so every site has to connect again. 1.0.6 then keeps the versioned sites and adds its own dump beside them; when this release starts on such a record it reads the dump, which holds what was done after the downgrade, and not the versioned sites, which 1.0.6 never showed and so could not have been disconnected.

The extension's background now starts each service under its own error handling. When the permission service fails to start, the contact book, notifications, phishing protection and the rest of the background still start, with no site connected and changes to site permissions refused. A storage read the browser refuses still stops start-up and reports that the stored settings could not be read.
