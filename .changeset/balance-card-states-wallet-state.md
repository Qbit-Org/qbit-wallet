'@qbit/wallet-state': minor

Show the balance card's loading and refreshing for what they are, and a failure only once it lasts. `useBalanceCardLogic` returns one `status` (`BalanceCardStatus`): `loading` with nothing known, `total` with the total known and the split loading, `ready`, `refreshing` over a shown value with its reason (a value read before, kept by the background or read before a lock; a read the user asked for; a retry after a read that brought no complete answer; or a background poll that has run longer than a second), `failed` over a shown value (with `totalCurrent`, whether a total shown without its split is the newest read's own) and `failed-empty` with nothing shown. The explorer's listing limit and a hidden balance stay as they were.

A failure shows only once two reads have failed since the trouble began, the later begun at least 10 seconds of the page being shown after the first, or once one has failed and 30 seconds of the page being shown have passed since it began, unless a read begun after the latest failure is still within the background's 20-second deadline. The trouble ends only when a read begun after the newest failed read settles without failing (complete, or incomplete without a failure); until then a failed state stays as it is, whatever a read in flight reports. The 30 s rule also waits for a read due within 3 seconds. A failure the card has shown stays until the trouble ends, through a page hidden and shown again and a return from another screen or account; on a failure not yet shown, older than the moment the card was last seen again (the page shown again, the card shown, the account or network selected), nothing shows until a read begun since has settled. A page shown again while a failure is counted reads at once, whatever the back-off, but not within 2 seconds of the previous read's start. Over a complete value the failure is one message (`balanceErrorText`); over a total whose outputs could not be read it is a note on the card and one under the split (`outputsUnreadText`, `outputsUnreadDetailText`), the split `--`, the total dimmed only when it is not the newest read's own (`totalDimmed`, `splitDimmed`). There is no Try again: `retryBalance` and `isRetryingBalance` are no longer returned, and `refreshBalance` reads at once under a failure.

A split unknown for a passing reason is loading, never `--`, and a split already shown stays, dimmed, under an answer of the same total that lacks it; an answer with another total shows that total, its split loading. A value shown as out of date is dimmed, and once dimmed it stays so until a fresh complete answer, for at least 400 ms; a read over a value shown undimmed dims it only after 400 ms (a user's refresh, the background's kept answer at open) or a second (a background poll), so a fast answer never flashes. A value from before a lock, a total alone included, is dimmed at once. `busy` (the ring, "Updating…") holds only while a read is in flight or due within a second; the value stays dimmed between reads. The poll tells the card each time its schedule changes, so the card renders again with the read due. The screen-reader text (`liveText`) says "Updating…" only while `busy` holds, never between reads. `statusText` is the full sentence ("Updating…", or how far a long read has got) and `statusShortText` the short form shown beside the refresh control. `utxoReadIncomplete`, `utxoReadIncompleteText`, `utxoReadIncompleteDetailText`, `balanceLoading`, `balanceLoadFailed` and `balanceRefreshFailed` are no longer returned.

The page reads an account once at a time. It opens with one balance read of the selected account, and it is the card's: the account updater hands its read to the card's poll, or the card takes the updater's read in flight as its own. At an unlock, a read in flight that began before the lock is not taken as the account's read. A read the user asks for joins the read in flight, and `useFetchBalanceCallback` (a refresh after a broadcast) reads after it, once for every caller meanwhile, or as soon as the card gives up on it (after 60 seconds), or, where no card gives up on it, once that read is 61 seconds old, a second after the card would have: then that read is no longer counted in flight and the refresh is asked for again by the same rules; `useStartBalanceReadCallback` begins a read at once, for the poll and the updater. The only read that may run beside another is one begun after the card gave up on a read that did not answer within 60 seconds, which counts as a failed read, or the read at an unlock. While no complete answer of the page's own is shown, the poll reads again 1.5, 3 and 6 seconds after a read that brought none, then at its usual pace and back-off; over a complete answer, the first failed read is followed by one more 2 seconds later, then the back-off. After a network switch, and on a card shown afresh after one (Settings, Switch network), the card starts no poll, asks for no kept answer and registers no reader until the selected account is known to be the new network's: another address, the account's own address on that network, or the end of the page's load of its accounts for that network (`useReloadAccounts` records it, succeeded or not, as `accountsLoaded`). The wait lasts 5 seconds at most, then the card reads the account selected. Meanwhile a refresh from elsewhere is refused, the account updater reads nothing and `canRefresh` is false. A load of the accounts that ends after a newer one is not recorded (`accountsLoaded.seq`), and the accounts the page opens with count as its first network's, so a card shown afresh after a switch made before any load ended waits too. A read in flight is taken as the card's own, or joined, only on the network shown.

The reducer keeps, beside the answers, the read in progress, its early total and count, the failed reads, the newest settled total, the last complete split (`balanceReadMap`) and the background's kept answer (`balanceKeptMap`), ordered by the same request ids, so that none replaces a newer answer and a kept answer never replaces one the page read itself. `balanceV2Map` and `balanceStatusMap` are written as before, so the send screen and the account switcher read what they did.

The balance card's breakdown is closed until the user opens it, and saved as they leave it: the `ui` state's `balanceDetailOpen` (action `setBalanceDetailOpen`) replaces `balanceDetailExpanded`, which builds up to 1.0.10 saved open by default and is not read, so every install starts closed once.
