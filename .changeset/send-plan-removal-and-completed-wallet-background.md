Two follow-ups to the send plans left open by #212 (#216, #217).

A removed wallet's unfinished plan stays stopped across a restart (#216). `removeKeyring` stores a record of the removal with the preferences before it removes anything (the wallet's slot and keyring type, and the accounts whose plans end) and is refused, nothing removed, when storage refuses it. The engine holds those accounts back from then until the record is forgotten on disk (`removalRecorded`, `endPlansOf(accounts, removal)`, `removalUndone`), and from the start of every later run (`restoreQbitSendPlanRemovals`, before `restoreQbitSendPlans`): nothing of their plans is signed, broadcast or advanced, and no plan begins for them. A record found at start-up is judged from the keyring at the first unlock (`QbitSendPlanEngineHost.judgeRemoval`): the slot empty, the plans are ended and the record forgotten once nothing of them is left; the wallet still there, the record is forgotten and the plans go on. A removal that fails once its last check passed is settled in the same run by a vault write from memory that is known to have landed, tried at the failure, at every unlock, after every successful removal and on a spaced timer. A forget that storage refuses is tried again, spaced from 15 seconds to 8 minutes for a removed wallet and at every tick for one that stays, with one log line per record. A record also waits for stored plan data the store cannot tell the account of that could be the plan of one of its accounts: an entry naming no address, of no network or of a network one of them is on. An entry naming only the address of one of its accounts is ended with that account's plans. Such data is now shown to an account it could be the plan of (it names nothing of an account, only that account's network, or only its address) that has no plan of its own, and that account can drop it. `holdNetworksIfIdle` counts an entry naming only an address on the networks that address is a P2MR address of. When a removal leaves something, `BUS_METHODS.QBIT_SEND_PLANS_NOT_ENDED` tells every wallet page once. The host interface gains `judgeRemoval` and `forgetRemoval`, and the engine `notEndedOf`; `QBIT_CHAINS` and `QBIT_CHAIN_TYPES` are exported.

A completed plan is followed until the transaction it names has confirmed, names the transaction that paid, and is settled again when the one it named is gone (#217). It keeps the engine's timer until the transaction it names has confirmed. Once that transaction has been missed for the grace in one run of the background and every transaction the payment spends from has been read since, what spends the payment's inputs decides: a version of the payment or a transaction paying the recipient the amount, found pending or confirmed, is named with its fee; with nothing spending them the plan reopens paused `interrupted` as a plan whose payment may still confirm, offering only Continue; with an input spent elsewhere it reopens `outputs_spent`. A plan that a transaction made elsewhere ever completed, by paying the recipient in the payment's place, remembers it until the transaction the plan names has confirmed: `paidBy` while it names that transaction, otherwise the last such transaction's id, amount and fee in the record's `extra.lastPaidBy` (no new record key; an entry that is stored but cannot be read counts, and names nothing). Such a plan offers a replan, a cancel or a merge fee raise again only once every input of its payment is spent by a transaction found confirmed; a spender that is only pending does not count. A payment input the explorer cannot answer for, the output of a merge it no longer holds included, is undecided and changes nothing. Until then it is completed naming that transaction, with dismiss, or, with every input unspent, paused offering only Continue. A plan no such transaction completed keeps the engine's standard. Each transaction, each parent's outspends and each spender is read at most once in 15 seconds whatever reads the plan, and the bound documented above `lookup` is restated.

A transaction the explorer names as spending an input of a plan's payment counts, on every path that identifies one, only when the explorer's own read of it lists that input. `QbitSendPlanEngineHost.transaction` returns the transaction's inputs as well (`QbitSendPlanSpenderRead`), from the same request. A read that does not list the input, or whose fee is not a non-negative safe integer, is no answer and is made again a tick later; an outspends answer with no entry for an input's output leaves a completed plan's input undecided.
