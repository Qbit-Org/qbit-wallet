Adding or editing a contact no longer clears its alias flag or silently replaces another contact. `updateContact`, `addContact` and `replaceContact` now take `isAlias` as optional: left unset, the stored entry keeps the alias flag of the entry already under the contact's address and chain, and a contact stored at an address with no entry gets `false`. A caller that sets `isAlias` still decides it. `addContact` is no longer the same as `updateContact`: it rejects with `ContactAlreadyExistsError` (`code` `CONTACT_ALREADY_EXISTS`) and changes nothing when a contact is already stored under the address and chain. `replaceContact` rejects the same way when another contact is stored under the new address and chain. Both check the store at the time of the write, so a screen that loaded before that contact was added cannot overwrite it. Address matching is exact, as in the stored key. Adding at an alias-only entry, editing a contact under its own address and chain, and moving it onto an alias-only entry all still save. `updateContact` still writes over the entry under its key. The wallet controller's `addContact` is now open to the wallet's own pages. The add and edit screen (`useEditContactScreenLogic`) no longer sends `isAlias`. It adds with `addContact`, moves with `replaceContact` and saves in place with `updateContact`. Before an add or a move, it checks the address, and in that case or when the contact book refuses, it shows "Another contact already has this address on this network" instead of saving. The message clears when the address changes.
