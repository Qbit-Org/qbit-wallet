Bound, keep and time-limit the reads behind an address's balance and outputs, so the balance of an account with thousands of outputs loads again (#36, #124). Since the explorer's output-listing limit was raised from 500 to 50,000 (#169), such an address took the per-output path: one `/tx` read per output, all at once, on every poll, which timed out in the browser's queue and failed the whole balance.

- Classification reads go through `QbitReadLimiter`: at most `QBIT_CLASSIFY_MAX_READS_IN_FLIGHT` (16) in flight to one explorer, funding transactions and unconfirmed ancestors together, first come first served, across every classification through the same client. The first failed funding-transaction read, or the caller's signal, stops the rest and cancels those in flight; an ancestor that cannot be read still only costs its output its own-change standing.
- `QbitFundingTxReads` keeps what a funding transaction's txid fixes about each output read (coinbase or not, value, script) per API client, for at most `QBIT_FUNDING_FACTS_MAX_OUTPOINTS` (65,536) outputs, least recently used first. A classification counts every kept output of its listing as used before it reads any afresh, so what it keeps pushes out other addresses' facts first: after a switch between two accounts that fit only one at a time, a poll reads only what the other account pushed out. It is kept and served only with the script of the address being read, served only for an output the fresh listing reports confirmed, and checked against the listed value; an unconfirmed output's transaction, the tip and every ancestor's confirmation are read afresh. A read of outputs that a send, sweep or plan spends serves a kept answer that the transaction is not a coinbase only for an output at least 1,000 blocks deep, as deep as a coinbase output must be to mature, and reads a younger one afresh, so one wrong answer from the explorer cannot let a send select an immature reward; the balance read, whose answer is only totals, serves it at any confirmed depth. Depth is measured from the listing's own newest block, and from the tip only for outputs that block cannot show deep, so a tip that cannot be read costs a fresh read of those outputs only. A poll of an address whose outputs did not change reads no transaction. The controller forgets it when the wallet locks, when its data is reset, when a keyring is removed and when the network changes, and nothing is served or kept while the wallet is locked.
- `getQbitSpendableBalanceV2` takes `QbitBalanceReadOptions` (`fundingTxReads`, `signal`, `deadlineMs`) and has one deadline for every read it makes, `QBIT_BALANCE_READ_DEADLINE_MS` (20 s). Once the address statistics are read, an output read that fails, or has not finished by the deadline, no longer rejects: the answer is the total with the split unknown and `utxoReadIncomplete: true` (and `utxoReadFailed: true` when the read failed, or ran out of time without keeping anything a later read is served, so a poll can back off), and the next read carries on from what was kept. With a shared reader, a balance read of an address whose listing names more outputs than the reader keeps facts of (`maxOutpoints`, 65,536 for the controller's) reads none of them one by one, and no tip: it answers at once with the total, the split unknown, `utxoReadIncomplete` and `utxoReadFailed`, so a poll backs off. A read of outputs for a send, sweep or plan still reads every one. It rejects when the statistics fail or do not answer by the deadline, and with the caller's reason when the caller's signal aborts. The over-limit answers (`utxoLimitExceeded`) are unchanged.
- `getQbitAddressUtxosClassified`, `getQbitAddressUtxosClassifiedPastLimit`, `getQbitAddressUtxosClassifiedFromHistory`, `readQbitAddressUtxos` and `getQbitAddressUtxos` take `QbitClassifyOptions` (`signal`, `fundingTxReads`). A read of outputs for a send still needs every funding transaction and fails when one cannot be read.

`getAddressSummary` is no longer a method the wallet's pages may call (`UI_CONTROLLER_METHODS`): nothing read its answer, and the balance refresh no longer asks for it.
