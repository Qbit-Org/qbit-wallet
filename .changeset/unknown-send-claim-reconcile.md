Stop holding the inputs of a wallet send with an unknown broadcast outcome until the background restarts (#339). Such a send's claim stayed held after its recovery screen was left, because the popup or tab, and its UI port, usually stay open. If the transaction never reached the node, its inputs stayed unavailable to every send long after lifecycle probes found it gone. If it landed, "Speed up" was refused with `Qbit transaction spends an input claimed by an approved send` before any signature was made. `qbitSendGuardService` now consumes a wallet-owned claim whose last dispatched broadcast was inconclusive into recent spends once its owner is gone, instead of keeping it held. Its owner is gone when the UI port disconnects, or when the recovery or confirm screen is left on any verdict other than a matching `not_sent`. The UI then calls the new `WalletController.abandonQbitSendBroadcastClaim` (`qbitSendGuardService.abandonBroadcastClaim`). Only the port that owns the claim can abandon it, and a claim no broadcast left the wallet for is released. The recent spends keep the inputs reserved until a lifecycle probe finds the transaction confirmed, or gone at least 10 minutes after it was recorded or last found pending, or for at most 24 hours. Meanwhile a fee bump of exactly that transaction may take them over. A lifecycle probe that finds a wallet-owned claim's transaction pending now consumes that claim into recent spends while its recovery screen is still open, and one that finds it confirmed drops it. This applies only to a claim registered before the probe was handed out. A probe miss never releases a held claim, nor drops the records of its transaction, so later probes keep watching for a transaction that lands late. `getAddressBalanceV2` now probes due spenders and counts outputs that a claim, an external signing lock or a recent broadcast reserves as unavailable, read after the outputs are listed. The balance card and the send screen therefore agree on what is available. `getQbitSpendableBalanceV2` takes an optional `readReserved` reader for this.
