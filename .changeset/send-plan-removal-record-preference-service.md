The preferences keep the records of wallet removals whose send plans may not all be ended yet (#216), in the optional `qbitSendPlanRemovals` field of the store: absent in a new store and in one saved before it existed, which reads as none, and dropped by `resetAllData`. `addQbitSendPlanRemoval(wallet, accounts)` appends a `QbitSendPlanRemovalRecord` (version 1: a random id, the wallet's slot and keyring type, and the accounts whose plans end, each a Qbit network and an address) and resolves with it once it is on disk; it rejects, the record not kept, when storage refuses the write or an account names no Qbit network. `getQbitSendPlanRemovals()` answers the records this version reads and, for the entries it cannot read (another version, a damaged one, a repeated id), how many there are and the accounts they name; such entries are kept as they are stored on every write. `forgetQbitSendPlanRemoval(id)` drops one record and rejects, the record kept, when storage refuses the write; it makes that one write itself and saves no restored value after a refusal, and the storage adapter's own deferred save follows a failed flush as for any preference. Changes to the list run one after the other, so two removals and a forget at the same time lose nothing. `QBIT_SEND_PLAN_CHAINS` is the set of networks a record may name.
