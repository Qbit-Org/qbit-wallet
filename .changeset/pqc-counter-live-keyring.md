'@qbit/keyring-service': patch

Refuse a PQC signature, counter change or keyring change that a lock and unlock cut off from the live keyrings. A signature resolved its keyring before it waited for the account's counter lock. If the wallet was locked and unlocked meanwhile, it reserved its counter on the old keyring, and `persistAllKeyrings` then wrote the keyrings the unlock had loaded, which lacked the reservation, under the new session's password. The signature was released anyway, and the next signature on that account reused its count. Every counter operation (`signPqcDigestWithCounter`, `signP2mrMessageWithCounter`, `signP2mrPsbtWithCounter`, their preflights, `reservePqcSignatureCounter`, `applyPqcSignatureCounterFloor`, `restorePqcSignatureCounterFloors`, `rearmQbitRestoreFloors` and `rearmQbitRestoreFloorsForKeyring`) now checks, under the counter lock, that its keyring is still live, and fails without signing when it is not. `persistAllKeyrings(changedKeyrings)` now requires the keyrings whose changes the write commits (`[]` for a write with no change) and fails, without writing, when any of them is no longer live once the write reaches the persistence queue, so a signature is released only after the vault holds its reservation. `addKeyring` and the create and import paths, `addNewAccount`, `removeAccount`, `changeAddressType`, `removeKeyring`, `syncQbitHdPath`, the receive-state and recovery-state mutations and the migration keyring pass the keyrings they change, as do their failure rollbacks, so they now fail instead of reporting success when a lock and unlock lost their change, and a rollback no longer rewrites a session it did not change. A signature whose reservation was saved before a lock now fails when its signature-cache write lands after the lock. These failures report `The keyring was locked, removed or replaced while this operation was in progress`. No vault field changes.
