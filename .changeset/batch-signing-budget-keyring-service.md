Sign every entry of an approved batch of P2MR PSBTs, in any order (#152). Each entry's budget is prepared before any entry signs, so once one entry had signed, `signP2mrPsbtWithCounter` refused every later entry that needed new signatures with "P2MR signature budget changed after approval". `KeyringService.openP2mrPsbtSigningBatch(address, inputs, { reuseScope? })` now opens a batch of the PSBTs one approval covers and returns its id and the whole batch's budget: every distinct signature its entries need, counted once, with the signatures remaining before the batch and after all its entries. `signP2mrPsbtWithCounter` takes the entry's `{ id, index }` as its fourth argument, in place of the `approvedBatchDuplicate` flag. The budget check then accepts exactly what the batch's other entries spent since the approval, measured under the counter lock: the counter they advanced and the signatures of this entry they signed and cached, as long as the batch stays within its approved total. It still refuses a signature made outside the batch, an entry signed twice, an entry of another account or PSBT, and any other drift. A batch whose entry failed partway counts what that entry spent. Restored-floor challenges are still checked per entry. `releaseP2mrPsbtSigningBatches(ids)` closes batches; locking the wallet closes them all, and past 64 open batches the oldest closes. An entry of a closed batch is checked as before.
