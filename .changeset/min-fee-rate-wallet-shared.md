Lower the minimum fee rate to qbit Core's relay floor (#182). `QBIT_MIN_RELAY_FEE_RATE` is 0.25 bits/vB, the node's `DEFAULT_MIN_RELAY_TX_FEE` of 250 sat/kvB, so `isQbitFeeRateWithinPolicy` and `assertQbitFeeRateWithinPolicy` accept rates from 0.25 to 10,000 bits/vB, and `qbitFeeForVsize`, `estimateQbitP2mrTransaction` and coin selection price a lower rate at 0.25. Fees are still rounded up, as the node rounds the fee it requires, so a one-input send at the floor pays 955 bits. The new `QBIT_FALLBACK_FEE_RATE` (1 bit/vB) is the rate to use when no live fee estimate is available, and `estimateQbitP2mrTransaction` still prices at 1 bit/vB when no `feeRate` is given. `assessQbitFee` and `qbitRecommendedFeeRate` accept a live recommendation from 0.25 bits/vB up. The `large_share_of_amount` check measures against that recommendation but never against less than `QBIT_FALLBACK_FEE_RATE`, which also stands in when there is no recommendation: change too small to keep, folded into a small send's fee, is a fixed amount of up to 2,854 bits, which below 1 bit/vB would by itself lift the send's rate past twice the recommendation. `above_recommended_rate` still compares with the recommendation as it is, so a 0.25 bits/vB recommendation flags 2.5 bits/vB. Breaking change: `QBIT_FEE_WARNING_RELAY_FLOOR_MULTIPLE` is removed. The `above_relay_floor` warning still fires at 100 bits/vB, now held in `QBIT_FEE_WARNING_RATE_BOUND`, and no longer moves with the floor, so a rate of 1 bit/vB or more, judged without a recommendation or against one of 1 bit/vB or more, is assessed as before.
