Quote and accept fee rates down to qbit Core's relay floor of 0.25 bits/vB (#182). On a mempool-qbit explorer `QbitWalletMempoolClient.getFeeTiers` now reads `/fees/precise`, whose rates for targets 1, 2 and 3 (`fastestFee`, `halfHourFee`, `hourFee`) go below 1 bit/vB, under the base URL, timeouts, signal and error types of `/fees/recommended`. Its reply is checked as `@qbit-oss/api` checks that of `/fees/recommended`, and its tiers must give an available quote. The precise read is sent once and has 5 seconds, headers and body, rather than the 15 seconds of the reply budget: when it fails in any way (any HTTP status, the network, a timeout, those 5 seconds, a body that is not JSON) or its tiers fail those checks, the call reads the whole-number tiers of `/fees/recommended` as before, with that read's retries, and rejects with that read's error when it fails too. Such a failure also makes that client read `/fees/recommended` directly, sending no precise read, for the next 10 minutes; the first call after them tries the precise read again, and a precise read that succeeds ends the wait. The caller's signal and deadline cover both reads and come first: an abort or a deadline of the caller's during the precise read ends the call with no further request and starts no wait. Esplora explorers are read as before. Every quoted rate below 0.25 bits/vB is raised to it, and every rate is rounded up to hundredths of a bit/vB, the resolution of the Custom rate input and of every screen that shows a rate; the 10,000 bits/vB ceiling applies to the rate as sent, and the check that a slower target costs no more than a faster one to the rounded rates. Whole-number tiers are quoted as before. Without a usable estimate every tier of the fee summary is `QBIT_FALLBACK_FEE_RATE`, 1 bit/vB, not the relay floor, and so is the rate a send-all or consolidation without a quote uses and the `recommendedFeeRate` of a PSBT review without a live quote. Sends, send-all, consolidation batches and a website's `sendQbit` `feeRate` are accepted from 0.25 bits/vB and refused below it with `feeRate must be between 0.25 and 10000 bits/vB`; `null`, strings and non-finite values are refused as before. A fee bump keeps its rules, deliberately above the node's 0.25 bits/vB incremental relay fee: a rate of at least the next whole bits/vB above the original's, or the quote's fastest rate when higher, and at least 1 bit per vbyte of added fee, so an original paying 0.25 or 0.75 bits/vB is replaced at 1.25 or 1.75 bits/vB at least. Its approval shows the original's and the replacement's rates rounded down to hundredths of a bit/vB, still with two decimals, so neither reads above the rate paid: an original paying 0.2452 bits/vB reads 0.24 bits/vB rather than 0.25, and one paying exactly 0.29 still reads 0.29. A recovery migration still prices no plan below 1 bit/vB: a live normal rate below it is raised to it, and `prepareQbitRecoveryMigration` now refuses a rate the caller gives below 1 bit/vB, before reading anything, with `feeRate must be between 1 and 10000 bits/vB`, which the fee policy no longer does.
